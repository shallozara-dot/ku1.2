Open-world driving around a sketch of Khulna University, Gollamari.

Welcome to the campus

Start at the Main Gate, hop into a car, CNG, rickshaw or bike, and take jobs. Your first job: carry a hot food order from the Cafeteria to Aparajita Hall or Begum Fazilatunnesa Mujib Hall before it goes cold. Hit students at speed or steal a moving ride and Campus Security comes after you.

About the map. The layout is a simplified sketch inspired by Khulna University at Gollamari, with the Moyur river on one side and Sher-e-Bangla Road (Khulna–Satkhira highway) along the front. As you enter the Main Gate, Aparajita Hall and Begum Fazilatunnesa Mujib Hall are at the far left corner, and Khan Jahan Ali, Khan Bahadur Ahsanullah and Bangabandhu halls are at the far right corner, along Hall Road beside the Moyur river. Khan Jahan Ali Hall, the Main Gate, the Central Library, Odommo Bangla and the thatched Cafeteria are drawn from reference photos. Positions and sizes are not surveyed and not to scale.
